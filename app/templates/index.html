How It Works
From raw postings to actionable intelligence
01

Collect

Fetch federal job postings from USAJOBS API and Adzuna every 2 hours, storing structured data in SQLite.

02

Normalize

Parse and clean salary ranges, locations, departments, series codes, and grade levels.

03

Enrich

Map extracted skills to ESCO classification — linking each skill to a type, category, and URI.

04

Visualize

Pre-compute analytics and serve interactive dashboards with charts, tables, and trends.
