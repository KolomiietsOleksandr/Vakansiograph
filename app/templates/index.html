Labor Market Intelligence Platform

VakansioGraph is a labor market intelligence platform that analyzes federal job postings from the USAJOBS API. We collect, normalize, and visualize data using ESCO (European Skills/Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) classification standards.

Built for career professionals, HR analysts, policy makers, and job seekers who want to understand the federal employment market in real-time.

Features

📊

Real-Time Analytics

Live tracking of federal job postings with automatic updates every 6 hours. Monitor market trends as they happen.

💼

Career Intelligence

Discover which skills are in highest demand. Compare salary ranges across grades and departments.

🗺️

Geographic Insights

See job distribution and remote work opportunities by state. Identify hotspots for federal employment.

🎓

ESCO Skills Mapping

Skills classified by European Skills Classification. Understand competence vs knowledge vs skill requirements.

💰

Salary Analysis

Comprehensive federal pay grade breakdown from GS-5 to SES. Min/max salary by department.

📈

Trend Forecasting

30-day posting trends by category. Identify emerging opportunities in your field.

🤖

Smart Job Matching

Get personalized job recommendations based on your skills, experience, and interests. Match profiles with relevant federal openings faster.

📄

Vacancy Change Tracking

Track edits in job postings over time. Detect salary updates, deadline changes, and newly added requirements automatically.
